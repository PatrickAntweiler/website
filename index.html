About

I am a PhD student in the Department of Mathematics at the University of Hamburg. My research lies at the interface of algebra, geometry, and mathematical physics, with a particular interest in the structures arising from homological methods and their applications to geometry.

Under the supervision of Prof. Julian Holstein, I study questions related to homological algebra, complex analytic geometry, non-commutative geometry, and mirror symmetry.

Research Interests

  • Homological Algebra
  • Generalised Complex Geometry
  • Deformation Quantization
